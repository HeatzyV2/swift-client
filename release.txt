## Changes (v1.5.0)

### 🧩 The Swift Client mod stays up to date by itself
- New **Swift Client mod** page in Settings: pick a channel (Stable, Beta or Local build), see the versions on your computer and go back to an older one in one click. The last three downloads are kept.
- New versions are downloaded in the background while the launcher is open, with a progress bar. Launching the game no longer waits for the network.
- The mod version is shown on the instance page.
- Mod 2.4.0: performance HUD, combat recap, streamer mode, auto profiles per server, chat filters, new menus.

### 💾 Safer worlds
- Worlds are backed up automatically before you play when they changed, and you can back one up or restore one from the Snapshots tab. Restoring never deletes your current world.

### 🩺 When something goes wrong
- The crash window now says what probably happened (out of memory, missing mod, wrong Java, broken mixin, graphics driver...) with a one-click fix when there is one.
- **Export diagnostics** (Settings, Advanced) makes a zip for support with secrets masked.
- An **Offline mode** bar appears when the Swift Platform cannot be reached; saved information is shown and playing still works.

### Also
- **Auto** button for memory, computed from your computer and your mods.
- **Join** button on the Servers page: launches your Swift Client instance and connects.

## Changes (v1.4.0)

### 🛰️ The launcher now follows the Swift Platform
- **Announcements**: info, success, warning and critical banners under the top bar, set from the admin panel. Close one and it stays closed.
- **Maintenance notice**: when the online services are down you are told once, then a banner stays. Your games still launch.
- **Home sections**: the Home can show a hero, a banner, text, quick links, servers and partners published from the admin, above the news.
- **Servers**: the Servers tab and the featured server of the Home panel are now managed online (the built-in list stays as a fallback).
- Works offline: the last answer is kept on your computer.

### 🔒 Anonymous statistics are opt-in
- Asked once, off by default, and switchable any time in Settings, Privacy. When on, only launch counts, versions and error categories are sent: no account, no IP address, no file path. When off, nothing is sent.

### 🧩 Swift Client mod 2.2.0
- Announcements and the maintenance notice show up as in-game toasts.
- A broken module can be switched off remotely without a new release.

### Also
- The Home avatar floats smoothly.
- Backend: the Swift Platform admin panel (French or English), versioned API, roles, audit log, webhooks and more.
