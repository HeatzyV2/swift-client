## Changes (v0.1.0)

### ✨ First Swift Client release
- New identity, interface and navigation: Home, Instances, Worlds, Screenshots, Skins, Settings.
- Home is built around launching: the selected instance, its version and loader, and one Play button.
- No telemetry, no analytics, no crash data sent anywhere.
- CurseForge, instance sharing by code and automatic updates are switched off until the Swift Client online service exists. Modrinth works as before.
- Backups and share packs made with Spectra Launcher can still be imported.
